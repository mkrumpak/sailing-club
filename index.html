Multihull Sailing in Saint Martin

Saint Martin Sailing Club (SMSC) is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing and our mission is to create exciting training and racing programs in the warm waters of Saint Martin.

Whether you are looking to join a training session or line up for a weekend of one design racing, SMSC provides the boats and the infrastructure for your adventure. We'd love to get you on the water, contact us!
