Multihull Sailing in Saint Martin

Saint Martin Sailing Club is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing. We offer exciting multihull training and racing programs in the warm, turquoise waters of Saint Martin.

Whether you are looking to join a training session or to line up for a weekend of one-design racing, SMSC provides the boats and everything else you need for your adventure. We'd love to get you on the water!
