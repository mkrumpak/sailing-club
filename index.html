Sailing in Saint Martin

Performance Multihull
Training and Racing

Saint Martin Sailing Club is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing. We offer multihull training and racing programs in the warm, turquoise waters of Saint Martin.

Whether you are looking to join a training session or to line up for one-design racing, SMSC provides the boats and everything else you need for your adventure. As a volunteer-run non-profit, we also run community sailing programs for residents. Get in touch, we'd love to get you on the water!
