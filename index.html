Multihull Sailing in Caribbean

SMSC is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing. Our focus is squarely on providing opportunities to learn about and master the technical mechanics of modern multihulls.

Whether you are here to experience flying a hull for the first time, to refine your wing mast rotation trim, secure a charter for a regatta or line up for weekend one design racing, SMSC provides the infrastructure, the boats, and the knowledge to push your limits.
