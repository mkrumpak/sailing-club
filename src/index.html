Multihull Sailing in Saint Martin

Saint Martin Sailing (SMSC) is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing. Our mission is to provide opportunities to learn and master the challenges of modern multihull sailing in the warm waters of Saint Martin.

Whether you are looking to join a training session or line up for a weekend of one design racing, SMSC provides the infrastructure, the boats, and the knowledge for your adventure. We'd love to get you on the water, join us!
