Charters

Regatta Charters

Traveling for a regatta doesn't mean you need to ship your boat. Visiting teams can charter one or more of our club maintained sailboats. Every boat is race-ready from the moment you step aboard, so you can focus on speed and tactics instead of logistics.

Charters are flexible. Book a single boat for one regatta, a multi-event charter if you're racing a series, or a full racing season charter if Saint Martin is becoming a regular stop on your calendar.

Who Charters With Us

Most of our charters come from Diam 24 and F18 teams flying in for a specific regatta or a short block of Caribbean racing. Many pair the boat with a Training Clinic and Race Prep session in the days beforehand. Some teams build a full winter or spring racing circuit around Saint Martin, chartering the same boat across multiple events. If that's closer to what you're planning, ask about season-long charter terms when you reach out.

Custom Events

Every group is different, so we build custom training and racing events around what you actually need. Whether you're bringing a squad down for a training camp, running your own clinic, or hosting a private regatta on the island, we can put together the right mix of sailboats, coaching, racing staff, infrastructure, and logistics to make it happen.

Tell us what you're planning, and we'll help shape it — which boats from our fleet fit your program, how much coaching and race support you need on the water, and what it takes to run smoothly. From a single custom coaching session to a multi-day team event, we'll build it around you.

Pricing

Diam 24 and F18 charters start at $650 per day, with a 3-day minimum. From there, you can tailor the package to your event — add racing sails, safety boat support, coaching, or other extras as needed.

Interested in chartering the ORC 50 for an island passage or a day on the water? Get in touch for current availability and pricing.

Getting Started

Most charters start with a quick conversation about your event, team size, and which boats fit your program. From there, we handle scheduling, prep, and logistics on our end, so you can focus on getting your team ready to race. Reach out with your dates and we'll confirm availability.
