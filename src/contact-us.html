Get in Touch

Whether you're planning a training trip, putting together a crew for a regatta, or just want to get out on the water, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact Us

Email: info@sxmsailingclub.com
WhatsApp: 1-721-587-1897

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us, especially if you're already on the island or have a time-sensitive question about an upcoming session.

Planning a Training Trip?

To help us put together the right program for you, let us know:

  • Which program you're interested in: the 4-Day Intensive Training & Racing session, the Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass, or a pre-regatta Training Clinic and Race Prep session
  • Your preferred dates and how flexible they are
  • How many sailors are in your group and roughly what your racing experience looks like
  • Which boats you'd like to sail: Diam 24, F18, or our ORC 50 catamaran

Racing and Charters

Coming to compete in the World Diam 24 Tour Championship, the Heineken Regatta, or one of our club races? We offer boat charters for visiting crews. Get in touch early, since boats book up ahead of major events. See the full racing calendar for dates.

Community Sailing

Our community sailing programs are free and open to anyone on the island, regardless of ability to pay. Reach out to find out when the next session runs and how to join.

Support the Club

SMSC is a non-profit association with no paid staff. Every dollar from our programs and donations goes directly into building and maintaining our boats and programs. If you'd like to donate or volunteer, we'd be glad to talk.
