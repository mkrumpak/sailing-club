About

Saint Martin Sailing Club is a non-profit association dedicated to nautical activities with a focus on high-performance multihull sailing in the Caribbean trade winds and the warm, turquoise waters of Saint Martin. That focus shows up in our fleet — Diam 24 trimarans, Formula 18 catamarans, and an ORC 50 catamaran — and in everything we build around it, from coached training programs to a full racing calendar. Whether you just started developing your multihull skills or a visiting crew chartering a boat for the season, SMSC gives you a real path onto the water.

Who Finds a Home Here

Maybe you're simply curious about multihulls and want to see what all the speed is about. Maybe you've sailed monohulls your whole life and are looking for something faster to test yourself against. Or maybe you're one of the many who've traded a colder home for trade winds and turquoise water, and you're looking for a place on the island that feels like yours. Whatever brought you here, there's room for you at SMSC.

Volunteers Welcome

Our community runs on more than sailing talent — it runs on people willing to pitch in. Race committee, event logistics, boat maintenance, coaching support: if you've got a few hours and some enthusiasm, there's a role for you, whether or not you're the one steering.

Why We Sail

We believe that time spent sailing changes the way people see the ocean. It builds a deeper appreciation for its power and beauty, and leaves an impression that lasts long after the sail ends. That connection to the water is exactly why raising environmental consciousness matters so much to us — we want every sailor who comes through SMSC to leave with a stronger sense of responsibility for protecting it. That's why SMSC exists, and why we're always looking to welcome more people into it.
