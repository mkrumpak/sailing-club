4-Day Intensive Training & Racing

SMSC's most complete training program — four consecutive days that take you from getting comfortable on a multihull to racing one, built as a single structured arc rather than four separate lessons.

What It Is

The 4-Day Intensive is a full, structured introduction to performance multihull sailing, combining coached technique-building with real racing by the end of the program. It's built around a deliberate progression: comfort first, skill-building second, race application last — so every day builds directly on the one before it instead of starting over.

Who It's For

This program works whether you're completely new to multihulls or you've sailed one before but want a focused block of time to actually improve, rather than logging occasional hours across a season. It's less suited to sailors who are already racing competitively and want to fine-tune specific details — that's better covered by the Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass.

What You'll Work On

Day one focuses on the platform itself: weight placement, balance, and basic maneuvers, until tacking, gybing, and holding a line stop feeling like individual events. Days two and three shift into real-time coached trim work — the repetition that turns a concept you understand into a habit you don't have to think about — with conditions and difficulty increasing as the days go on. Day four puts everything into racing, using what's been built over the previous three days under actual competitive pressure.

Format

Four consecutive days, structured to build progressively rather than repeat the same lesson at different speeds. Conditions and pacing adjust to the group, but the arc — comfort, skill-building, racing — stays the same.

If You're Not Sure This Is Right

If you're already racing competitively and want to refine specific technique rather than build a full skill set, the Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass is likely the better fit. If you've got a specific regatta coming up and just need final preparation, Training Clinic & Race Prep is built for exactly that.

Ready to Start?

Four consecutive days of coached, progressively harder sailing does more for your skills than the same hours spread across a season of occasional outings.
