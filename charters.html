Charters

Regatta Charters

Traveling for a regatta doesn't mean you need to ship or borrow a boat. Visiting teams can charter one or more of our club-maintained sailboats — including our Diam 24 trimarans, Formula 18 catamarans, and ORC 50 catamaran — race-ready from the moment you step aboard, so you can focus on speed and tactics instead of logistics.

Charters are flexible. Book a single boat for one regatta, a multi-event charter if you're racing a series, or a full racing season charter if Sint Maarten is becoming a regular stop on your calendar.

Custom Events

Every group is different, so we build custom training and racing events around what you actually need. Whether you're bringing a squad down for a training camp, running your own clinic, or hosting a private regatta on the island, we can put together the right mix of sailboats, coaching, racing staff, infrastructure, and logistics to make it happen.

Tell us what you're planning, and we'll help shape it — which boats from our fleet fit your program, how much coaching and race support you need on the water, and what it takes to run smoothly on the ground. From a single custom coaching session to a multi-day team event, we'll build it around you.

Pricing

Diam 24 and F18 charters start at $650 per day, with a 3-day minimum. From there, you can tailor the package to your event — add racing sails, safety boat support, coaching, or other extras as needed.

Interested in chartering the ORC 50 for an island passage or a day on the water? Get in touch for current availability and pricing.
