Training

Which Program Is Right for You?

New to multihulls, or want the most complete experience? The 4-Day Intensive combines coaching with fleet racing. Already comfortable in the boat and want to sharpen a specific skill? The Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass goes deep on trim and rig setup alone. Racing a specific regatta and want to arrive ready? A Training Clinic and Race Prep session ahead of your event is built around exactly that goal. Not sure which fits? Tell us your experience level and what you're working toward, and we'll point you to the right session.

4-Day Intensive Multihull Training & Racing Session

Take your multihull performance to the next level in the trade winds of Saint Martin. Designed for Diam 24 and Formula 18 sailors, this four-day intensive program bridges the gap between technical theory and high-stakes fleet racing — coaching sessions on the water are followed by fleet racing, so what you learn gets tested and refined the same day.

It's also a natural fit for competitive monohull sailors looking to cross-train on something faster, J/70 crews especially, since so much of the racecraft — starts, boat-on-boat tactics, sailing to target speed — carries straight back to the home fleet. Whatever boat you're coming from, the four days are built around your team's specific goals rather than a fixed curriculum, so you leave with real, race-tested improvement.

Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass

This clinic focuses on the theoretical and practical application of multihull sail trim. We cover mainsail twist profiles, jib lead angles to manage the slot, rig tuning, mast rotation and sailing to target speed and angle.

Training Clinic and Race Prep

We run these clinics before all Caribbean Championship Regattas and adjust the curriculum to the goals of each team. We typically cover boat preparation and tuning, starts, sailing to targets, maneuvers, tactics, and many other topics.
