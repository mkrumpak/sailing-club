Training Programs

Four main programs: the 4-Day Intensive Training & Racing session, the Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass, and pre-regatta Training Clinic and Race Prep sessions. Each is built around a different goal, from building foundational multihull skills to sharpening race-day performance right before an event.

The 4-Day Intensive Training & Racing session is our most complete program — several days combining coached training with real racing, best if you want a full, structured introduction or a deep block of time on the water. Training Clinic and Race Prep sessions are shorter and timed right before a regatta, designed to sharpen specific skills and boat setup ahead of race day rather than build skills from the ground up.

Sailors who already have multihull racing experience and want to refine the details — the habits that separate a solid club racer from a championship-level crew. It's not the place to start if you're new to multihull sailing.

Our core programs are small-group and coached. If you're looking for something more tailored, email us at info@sxmsailingclub.com and we can talk through options.

The Fleet

Diam 24 trimarans, Formula 18 (F18) catamarans, and an ORC 50 catamaran — a real range, from nimble two-person multihulls to a much bigger boat with a different set of dynamics.

The F18 is a beach-cat style catamaran and a common starting point for sailors newer to multihulls. The Diam 24 is a faster, more technical trimaran built for performance racing — light and quick to accelerate, and the boat behind our Diam 24 training and the World Diam 24 Tour Championship. The ORC 50 is the biggest boat in the fleet, a larger catamaran that handles differently from the smaller boats.

Planning Your Trip

Trade winds are steadiest from December through May, generally in the 15-to-25-knot range. That's our high season and the most reliable stretch for consistent training and racing conditions.

IPrincess Juliana International Airport has direct flights from major U.S. hubs including New York, Miami, Boston, Atlanta, Philadelphia, and Charlotte, plus direct service from Toronto and Montreal — no multi-leg routing required from most major cities.
