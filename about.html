About

Saint Martin Sailing Club (SMSC) is a non-profit association dedicated to nautical activities, with a particular focus on multihull sailing. We were founded on a simple mission: to give sailors and everyone else in our community who is interested in experiencing sailing the opportunity to learn high-performance sailing in the trade winds and turquoise waters of Sint Maarten and neighbouring islands.

That focus on performance shapes everything we do. Our fleet includes Diam 24 trimarans, Formula 18 catamarans, and an ORC 50 catamaran, and we build our training programs, race clinics, and club racing calendar around them. Whether you're a member developing your multihull skills or a visiting crew chartering a boat for the season, SMSC gives you a real path onto the water.

We believe that time spent sailing changes the way people see the ocean. It builds a deeper appreciation for its power and beauty, and leaves an impression that lasts long after the sail ends.

That connection to the water is exactly why raising environmental consciousness matters so much to us. We want SMSC to help sailors understand the threats facing ocean health today, and to leave every sail with a stronger sense of responsibility for protecting it. That's the experience we set out to create — and it's why SMSC exists, and why we're always looking to welcome more people into it.

New Members Welcome

You don't need to be a seasoned racer to find a home at SMSC. Maybe you're simply curious about multihulls and want to see what all the speed is about. Maybe you've sailed monohulls your whole life and you're ready to feel something faster. Or maybe you're a Northerner who's traded snow boots for trade winds and is looking for a place on the island to call your own. Whatever brought you here, there's room for you at SMSC.

Once you're in, we make it easy to actually get out on the water — not just watch from the dock. You'll have access to our full fleet of multihulls, hands-on coaching to build real skill on them, and training programs designed for wherever you're starting from. But more than the boats, what keeps members coming back is the community: sailors who will welcome you in, show you the ropes, and make Sint Maarten feel like home.

Volunteers Welcome

Our community runs on more than sailing talent — it runs on people willing to pitch in. If you'd like to get involved beyond your own time on the water, SMSC is always looking for volunteers to help run events, support our training clinics and regattas, and keep the club going behind the scenes. Race committee, event-day logistics, and helping host visiting crews are just a few of the ways to contribute — many of these roles don't require sailing experience, just a willingness to help out. It's one of the best ways to get to know the club from the inside and become part of what makes SMSC what it is. If you're interested in volunteering, get in touch and we'll help find the right fit.
