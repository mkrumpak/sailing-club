Training Clinic & Race Prep

A shorter, pre-regatta program built to get you and your boat genuinely race-ready — not a place to learn multihull sailing from scratch, but the right last step before a specific event.

What It Is

Training Clinic and Race Prep sessions are timed right before a regatta and focused on final preparation: boat setup, tuning, and sharpening specific skills for the conditions and competition you're about to face, rather than building technique from the ground up. It's the difference between general training and getting a specific boat and crew ready for a specific starting line.

Who It's For

This program suits sailors who already have some multihull comfort — whether from SMSC's own 4-Day Intensive, another program, or experience elsewhere — and have a regatta coming up they want to be properly prepared for. Visiting sailors aiming for a spot in SMSC's club racing calendar or a World Diam 24 Tour Championship stop often use this as the bridge between arriving on the island and actually being ready to race.

What You'll Work On

Coverage adjusts to what a specific event and boat actually need — that might mean rig tuning and finding fast settings, sharpening starts and mark roundings, or drilling the maneuvers most likely to matter in the conditions you're expecting. The focus stays practical and specific to the racing ahead, not general skill-building.

Format

A shorter, more flexible format than the 4-Day Intensive, scheduled around your event rather than a fixed multi-day arc. Reach out with your dates and the regatta you're prepping for, and the session gets built around that.

If You're Not Sure This Is Right

If you've never sailed a multihull before, this isn't the place to start — the 4-Day Intensive builds that foundation properly. If you're an experienced racer looking to sharpen trim technique generally rather than prep for one specific event, the Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass is the better long-term investment.

Got a Regatta Coming Up?

Tell us the event and your dates, and we'll help you figure out exactly what kind of prep will actually make a difference before the start gun.
