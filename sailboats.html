Sailboats

Diam 24

The Diam 24 is the ultimate one-design sport multihull. Featuring a rotating wing mast, high-volume piercing bows, and blistering acceleration, it rewards precise driving and acurate sail trim with a thrilling sailing experience.

F18

The F18 box rule creates one of the most competitive, developmental multihull classes in the world. These boats are the proving ground for twin-trapeze mechanics, apparent wind sailing, and high-speed tactical decision-making.

ORC 50

ORC 50 is a fast cruising catamaran that combines sailing performance, responsivness and comfort. Built with advanced composite materials, the ORC 50 is capable of clocking 350+ mile daily runs, top speed of 30+ knots, positioning it at the very peak of production performance catamarans.
