Racing

Our fleet's racing calendar includes six World Tour Caribbean regattas and several other races. Some events are short, high-tempo Grand Prix style races; others are long-distance races with offshore passages between Sint Maarten and neighboring islands like St Barth and Anguilla, testing endurance as much as boat speed. And a few are marquee, world-class events — none bigger than the Heineken Regatta, one of the Caribbean's best-known regattas.

Whether you're training for your first Grand Prix or building toward a full championship season, the calendar below lays out what's coming up — where, when, and how to find out more.
