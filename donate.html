Donate

SMSC runs on trade winds, volunteer hours, and a fleet that needs constant upkeep — and increasingly, on the generosity of people who believe what we're building is worth funding. If you've sailed with us, watched from the beach during a regatta, or just believe Saint Martin should stay one of the best places in the Caribbean to learn to sail, this is where that support goes.

What Your Support Makes Possible

Some of what SMSC does is only possible because of donors. Our community sailing programs are open to anyone on the island who wants time on the water, whether or not they can afford training fees or a membership — and programs like that don't pay for themselves. Every gift helps keep that door open, alongside the ordinary costs of running a fleet: maintenance, safety equipment, and the training and racing calendar that brings people back season after season.

How to Give

Reach out to us and we'll walk you through how to make a gift — whether that's a one-time donation or ongoing support.
